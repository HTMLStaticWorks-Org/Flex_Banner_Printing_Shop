03 // QUALITY ASSURANCE

FOUR-POINT PRE-PRESS & PRINT CHECK.

Every print job passes through four structured checkpoints so your finished material matches your approved layout specifications.

CHECK // 01

Dimension & Bleed Verification

We verify aspect ratios, safe margins, eyelet clearance zones, and fold lines before queuing your artwork for production.

CHECK // 02

CMYK Profile Calibration

Colour profiles are matched to the selected substrate—compensating for matte, gloss, or backlit light diffusion characteristics.

CHECK // 03

Substrate & Lamination Bonding

Prints receive proper ink curing time before cold/thermal lamination or sunboard pasting to prevent edge lifting or bubbles.

CHECK // 04

Final Edge & Hardware Inspection

Every banner seam, eyelet spacing, standee cassette tension, and die-cut sheet is checked prior to customer collection or dispatch.
