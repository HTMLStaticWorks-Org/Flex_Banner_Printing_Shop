01. What file formats are best for printing?
We recommend print-ready PDF, AI, EPS, CDR, PSD, or high-resolution TIFF/PNG files in CMYK colour mode with all fonts converted to outlines/curves.
02. What if I do not have a print-ready design?
Simply select “No (Need Design Support)” on our Order Enquiry Form and share your rough text, logo, or concept. Our studio team will assist with layout preparation.
03. How do I choose between Frontlit Flex, Star Flex, and Vinyl?
Standard Frontlit Flex suits short-to-medium promotions; Star Flex offers heavier tear resistance for large outdoor hoardings; Laminated Adhesive Vinyl is best for smooth glass, acrylic, and indoor sunboard panels.
04. How is custom printing priced?
Large-format banners, vinyl, and signage are calculated per square foot (Width × Height) based on material grade and finishing, while cards, brochures, and standees are quoted per unit or batch.
